Spencer Bot - Personal Budgeting

Spencer Bot is a private, personal-use budgeting tool built by Spencer Wright for his own bank accounts only.

It uses Plaid to read his own transactions and balances (read-only) to build pay-period budgets and spending insights. It cannot move money.

  • Single user. There are no customers and no other users.
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties.
  • Data is deleted on request by removing the Plaid connection and local files.

Privacy

This is a private, personal-use tool. The only person whose data it accesses is its owner, Spencer Wright, who consents by linking his own accounts through Plaid. Transactions and balances are read (read-only) solely to build personal pay-period budgets and spending insights. There is a single user, no customers, and no third-party sharing or selling of data. Data is deleted on request by removing the Plaid connection and local files.
